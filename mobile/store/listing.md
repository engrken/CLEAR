## Full description (≤ 4000 characters)

```
CLEAR System (Centralized Liquidation of Expenses & Audit Records) keeps the books for student organizations and departments at Filamer Christian University, and lets students check their clearance without lining up at the office.

FOR STUDENTS
• Pick your organization and look up your clearance with your student ID.
• See at a glance whether you are Cleared or On hold for the current term.
• Show your clearance QR code; officers can verify it live.
• Paid by GCash, Maya or bank transfer? Attach a screenshot of your receipt and the reference number, and the treasurer reviews it, with no need to bring a printout.

FOR ORG OFFICERS
• Sign in with your officer email and a one-time code. No password to remember or share.
• Dashboard: collections, outstanding balances and fund totals for the active academic year and term.
• Ledger: every payment, remittance and adjustment, newest first.
• Record a cash payment against a student and fee, with the official receipt (OR) number.
• Review online payment proofs: check the receipt image, then verify it with the OR number or mark it invalid.
• Access follows your role (admin, treasurer, auditor or viewer), the same as on the web.

BUILT FOR ACCOUNTABILITY
CLEAR works on the same records as the CLEAR web app, so officers, advisers and auditors see one set of books. Every entry keeps who recorded it and when. Connections are encrypted, sign-in codes expire after five minutes, and an officer's access ends as soon as their account is deactivated.

Officer accounts are created by your organization's CLEAR administrator. Students do not need an account.
```

> Before publishing, confirm with FCU (Office of Student Affairs or the org adviser) that the app may use the university's name. If it is not an official FCU app, add this last line to the description: `CLEAR System is an independent tool used by FCU student organizations and is not an official app of Filamer Christian University.` See [NOTES.md](NOTES.md).
